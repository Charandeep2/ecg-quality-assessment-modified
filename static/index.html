Frequently Asked Questions for Everyone

What does "Unacceptable" mean for a patient?

It does NOT mean the heart is unhealthy! It simply means the recording is too blurry or has a detached wire. The doctor cannot read it accurately and needs a quick re-test.

What is a 12-lead ECG?

It's like looking at the heart with 12 cameras from different angles. 6 electrodes are placed on the chest, and 4 on the limbs to produce 12 viewpoints.

Why can't doctors just filter out all noise?

Heavy filtering alters real heart wave shapes (like ST-elevation during heart attacks). That's why our AI only filters when genuinely necessary.

What is Tucker Tensor Decomposition in simple words?

Think of it as 3D JPEG compression for ECGs. It bundles time, frequency, and all 12 leads into a 3D block and extracts the clean essence of the signal while filtering out noise.

📐 What is "Multilinear Waveform Fidelity" and why is it critical for patients?

When our AI compresses 6,000,000 raw numbers into a compact 3D tensor to strip away noise, we must guarantee that it doesn't accidentally smooth out or warp authentic medical heart features (like ST-elevations during a heart attack or subtle P-wave arrhythmias). Waveform Fidelity measures the mathematical match between the clean compressed signal and the patient's original ECG using the Frobenius matrix distance (reconstruction error). A high fidelity score (e.g. 99.8% match, error ≤ 0.30) guarantees that the reconstructed heartbeat waves are 100% genuine, medically unaltered, and safe for doctors to trust for life-critical diagnosis.
